Rules

  • Two players alternate turns.
  • Roll the cowrie (dice) and move forward.
  • Land at the base of a stair to rise. Land on a serpent head to fall.
  • You must roll the exact number to reach square 100.

The Journey

The Journey

Vaikuntapali is more than a game. It is a pilgrimage across virtues and vices, where every rise and fall reflects a moral truth. Reach square 100 to ascend to Vaikuntha.

Virtues & Vices

Ladders are virtues that lift the soul. Serpents are vices that pull it down. Each encounter offers a teaching for the path ahead.
