Classic Indian Board Game

Vaikuntapali

A moral journey of ascent and descent. Rise through virtues, fall through vices.

Stairs (Ladders) Virtues lift the soul.
Serpents (Snakes) Vices bring the fall.
Vaikuntha Reach 100 to win.
The board is numbered in a winding path (1 to 100). Each stair and serpent carries a moral teaching.

Player 1 begins
-

Virtues and Vices

Traditional Vaikuntapali pairs each rise or fall with a lesson. The version below uses classic Sanskrit ideals in romanized form.

Rules

  • Two players alternate turns.
  • Roll the cowrie (dice) and move forward.
  • Land at the base of a stair to rise. Land on a serpent head to fall.
  • You must roll the exact number to reach square 100.
